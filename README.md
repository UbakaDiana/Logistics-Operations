# Logistics Operations

## Project Overview

This project explores logistics operations across three key business areas: driver performance, customer revenue, and safety.

Using SQL for data preparation and analysis and Power BI for visualization, I developed an interactive dashboard to examine operational efficiency, revenue performance, customer activity, and safety-related costs.

The dashboard brings these areas together to provide a consolidated view of logistics operations and help identify patterns that may require further investigation.

## Project Objectives

* Evaluate driver performance using trip completion, mileage, and revenue metrics.
* Analyze customer activity, annual revenue potential, and account status.
* Examine vehicle damage, cargo damage, and incident patterns.
* Identify high-performing drivers, valuable customer segments, and areas with significant safety-related costs.
* Present key operational metrics in an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Dashboard Sections

### 1. Driver Performance Dashboard

This section evaluates driver activity, trip performance, and revenue contribution.

#### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Drivers: 150
* Total Trips: 85,410
* Trip Completion Rate: 98%
* Total Miles: 120 million
* Total Revenue: 257 million

#### Business Questions

* Which drivers complete the highest number of trips?
* Which drivers generate the most revenue?
* How does trip completion vary across the reporting years?
* Which states generate the highest revenue?
* Which drivers are associated with the highest vehicle damage costs?

### 2. Customer Report Dashboard

This section focuses on customer activity, revenue potential, account status, and credit terms.

#### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Customers: 200
* Active Customers: 168
* Total Annual Revenue Potential: 538 million
* Average Credit Term: 38 days

#### Business Questions

* How many customers are active compared with the total customer base?
* Which customer freight types contribute the most annual revenue potential?
* How is annual revenue potential distributed across active and inactive accounts?
* How do average credit terms compare across customer types?
* Which customer types have the highest annual revenue potential?
* How has annual revenue potential changed over time?

### 3. Safety Report Dashboard

This section examines reported incidents, vehicle and cargo damage costs, and claim amounts.

#### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Incidents: 170
* Vehicle Damage Cost: 1.6 million
* Cargo Damage Cost: 1 million
* Claim Amount: 2.7 million

#### Business Questions

* Which cities record the highest vehicle damage costs?
* Which cities account for the highest cargo damage costs?
* How are incidents distributed across cities?
* Which incident types occur most frequently?
* Which states record the highest number of incidents?
* How have incident counts changed over the reporting years?

## Key Business Value

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of logistics operations, allowing stakeholders to explore:

* Driver performance: Compare trip activity, revenue contribution, and vehicle damage costs.
* Customer performance: Understand customer activity, revenue potential, and credit terms.
* Safety performance: Monitor incident patterns and damage-related costs across locations and incident types.
* Operational trends: Compare performance across reporting periods to identify areas that may need attention.

These insights can support further investigation into operational efficiency, customer account management, and safety improvement opportunities.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how SQL and Power BI can be used together to analyze a multi-dimensional logistics dataset.

By organizing operational data into driver performance, customer reporting, and safety analysis, I created a dashboard that makes key metrics easier to monitor and compare.

The project also demonstrates the importance of connecting technical data analysis with practical business questions to produce reporting that supports decision-making.
